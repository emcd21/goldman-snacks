Which topics on this site help with which ACCA exams, so your revision counts towards your degree and your ACCA qualification at the same time.

Check the details with ACCA. The syllabus for each exam, and any exemptions your degree gives you, are set by ACCA and can change. Use this map as a guide to where the site overlaps with each exam, then check the current syllabus and your exemptions on the ACCA website or in your myACCA account.
